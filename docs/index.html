Engineered for Precision & Atmosphere

Combining modern desktop aesthetics with scientific meteorological depth.

🌧️

60 FPS Particle Engine & 20 Dynamic Skies

Falling rain, drifting snow, twilight mist, and twinkling stars are composited directly by GPU shaders, smoothly reacting to changing wind speeds and precipitation volume over 20 panoramic atmospheric skies.

🌊

Proactive Disaster & Flood Alerts

Intelligent background engine continuously analyzes heavy rain accumulation, flash flood hazards, extreme heatwaves, and gale-force wind bursts with native Linux desktop toasts.

☀️

Solar Transit & Moon Phase Tracking

Interactive Bézier arc tracking live solar elevation and daylight progression alongside real-time moon illumination percentages and next full moon countdowns.

💨

Live Wind Compass & European AQI

Bearing compass with peak gusts, particulate matter concentration (PM2.5 / PM10), Magnus-Tetens dew point, barometric pressure, and visibility clarity.

🐧

Native Wayland & Single-Instance IPC

Zero tearing with `PassThrough` HiDPI fractional scaling on 2K/4K displays, local IPC socket preventing duplicate windows, and background system tray integration.

🚀 Get Started with Caelum

Run directly on Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian, Arch Linux, Manjaro, openSUSE, or any Linux desktop.

1

Clone Repository

Clone the source tree from GitHub to your preferred local directory.

2

Execute run.sh

Self-bootstraps an isolated virtual environment and installs Qt6 requirements automatically.

3

Add to App Menu

Run ./install_desktop.sh to integrate Caelum seamlessly with your system dock and launcher.
